CAAST is a research tool, not an operational forecast. It identifies historical AFDs with similar language to recent forecasts — it does not predict weather. Signal levels reflect past pattern matching, not current threat assessment. Always rely on official NWS forecasts, watches, and warnings for weather decisions.

Signal Legend

HIGH — strong severe pattern

LOW — typical pattern

About

CAAST analyzes recently issued Area Forecast Discussions across all 122 NWS WFOs and finds historical analogs from a 22-year archive (5.2M sections). Signal levels compare today's analog severe weather rate against each office's climatological baseline. Click a CWA to see analogs.

Personal research project, not an official NWS/NOAA product.
