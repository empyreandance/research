Maps & Tools

  • Daily Map
    Temperature Calendar Anomaly
    A daily-updating map that reframes temperature anomalies as time rather than degrees. Instead of showing "5°F above normal," it shows how many days ahead or behind the normal temperature schedule each location is. If the forecast high for July 1 matches the climatological normal for July 15, that spot shows +14 days — two weeks ahead of schedule.

    This is an independent project and is not an official product of the National Weather Service or NOAA. Data: NWS NDFD forecast highs / RCC-ACIS 1991–2020 daily normals.

  • Interactive Tool
    California Traffic Cameras by Elevation
    An interactive map of Caltrans traffic cameras sortable by elevation, useful for monitoring snow levels, chain controls, and road conditions across mountain passes during winter storms.
